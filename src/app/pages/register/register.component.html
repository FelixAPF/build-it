1. Nature of the Service

BuildIt provides a software-as-a-service (SaaS) matching platform. BuildIt is not an employer, a joint employer, a contractor, a subcontractor, or a personnel placement agency. The use of the Platform does not create an employment relationship between BuildIt and any Business or Worker.

2. Responsibilities of the Business (Employer)

When a Business selects a Worker through the Platform, the Business assumes the role of the sole legal employer of that Worker. The Business is exclusively responsible for complying with the Act respecting labour relations, vocational training and workforce management in the construction industry (Act R-20) and the CCQ. The Business is exclusively responsible for the payment of wages, union dues, and CNESST premiums.

3. Responsibilities of the Worker (Employee)

You understand that by accepting a job requirement, you are entering into a temporary employment relationship directly with the Business, not with BuildIt. You represent and warrant that you hold valid CCQ competency cards for the trades you select.

4. Fees and Payments

BuildIt charges a non-refundable matchmaking fee for the use of the software. BuildIt does not process worker wages or facilitate the transfer of payroll funds.

5. Indemnification

You agree to indemnify and hold harmless BuildIt from any claims, CCQ fines, union grievances, or damages arising out of your use of the Platform.
